WHAT YOU'LL WORK ON

More Than Just Chords.

Guitar is more than remembering chord shapes. The lessons are designed around the different skills that come together when you actually play.

01

Chords

Learn chord shapes, transitions, progressions, and their practical use.

02

Rhythm

Develop timing, strumming control, consistency, and rhythmic feel.

03

Technique

Work on the physical skills that help make your playing cleaner and smoother.

04

Tabs

Understand guitar tabs and use them to approach songs and musical ideas.

05

Songs

Apply concepts through songs and turn lessons into actual playing.

06

Practice

Build a more focused practice approach instead of wondering what to work on.
