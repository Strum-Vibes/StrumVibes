Privacy Policy.

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how StrumVibes collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with our website and services.

Last Updated · August 2026

01. Introduction

Welcome to StrumVibes. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you choose to share with us.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information we may collect through our website, how that information may be used, and the steps we take to keep it protected.

02. Information We Collect

When you contact StrumVibes or submit an enquiry through our website, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name
  • Phone number or WhatsApp number
  • Email address
  • Course or learning preference
  • Information you voluntarily provide in your enquiry

03. How We Use Your Information

Information provided to StrumVibes may be used to respond to enquiries, understand your learning requirements, communicate with you about guitar classes, and provide information related to our services.

We do not use the information you provide for purposes unrelated to your enquiry unless we have a lawful basis to do so.

04. Information Protection

We take reasonable steps to protect the information submitted through our website against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

05. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services or technologies to support website functionality, analytics, hosting, forms, or other features.

Such third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

06. Cookies

StrumVibes may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary to improve website functionality, understand website usage, or provide a better browsing experience.

You may be able to manage or disable cookies through your browser settings.

07. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to communicate with you, or where required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

08. Your Choices

You may choose not to provide certain information. However, this may prevent us from responding to your enquiry or providing certain services.

If you would like to ask about the information we may hold about you or request its correction or deletion, you can contact StrumVibes.

09. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, website, or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised update date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact StrumVibes.
