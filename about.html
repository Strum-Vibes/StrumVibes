THE BEGINNING

I Started Learning.

In 2021, I finally decided to learn guitar. I started taking classes with one simple motivation — I genuinely wanted to learn, play, and understand the instrument.

The beginning wasn't always easy. My fingers hurt, progress sometimes felt slow, and there were moments when I felt discouraged. But I never quit.

Over that first year, from 2021 to 2022, I improved far more than I had imagined. More importantly, that first year made me realise how much I genuinely enjoyed playing guitar.

THE GAP

Learning Wasn't The Hard Part. Knowing What To Learn Was.

After leaving my classes, I wanted to keep improving. But suddenly, I didn't really know how. What should I learn next? How do I improve my technique? What should I practise? Am I even learning the right things?

Confusion

For around 5–6 months, I explored different channels, websites, tutorials and resources trying to figure out how to improve.

And eventually, I realised that I wasn't lacking information. I was lacking direction.

THE REALISATION

I Didn't Need More Information.
I Needed The Right Direction.

Those months of searching taught me something important. Getting better at guitar isn't simply about practising more. It's about knowing what to learn, when to learn it, how to practise it, and how everything connects.

2025 · THE IDEA

So I Built The Path I Wish I Had.

In 2025, the idea of StrumVibes started taking shape. The idea was simple — create a place where beginners and aspiring guitarists don't have to spend months wondering where to start, what to learn next, or how to overcome the point where they've become stuck.

THE PURPOSE

No One Should Have To Figure It All Out Alone.

I believe there shouldn't be unnecessary hurdles for someone who genuinely wants to pick up a guitar and enjoy playing.

And for someone who is struggling, stuck, or simply doesn't know what to do next, the right guidance can make all the difference.

I spent months trying to figure that out myself.

StrumVibes exists so you don't have to.
